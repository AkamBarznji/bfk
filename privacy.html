Clear information about the data used by this website.

To be confirmedThis is a preview notice, not a final UK GDPR privacy policy. The legal entity, contact service and data-handling arrangements must be confirmed before public launch.

This design preview

The contact form is a demonstration. It checks example information in your browser only and does not submit messages, send email or store the form’s contents. Please do not enter sensitive or confidential information.

This website includes no analytics, advertising scripts, embedded social feeds or third-party font requests. Its photographs and fonts are served with the website. The hosting service may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, and access controls may use essential authentication.

The future contact service

The live form is intended to collect your name, organisation if supplied, email address, enquiry type and message, for responding to your enquiry and managing relevant correspondence. The published policy will explain who controls that information, the applicable lawful basis, who receives it and how long it is kept.
