Discover BFK

BFK is formed to act as a vital bridge for the United Kingdom and Kurdistan partnership, helping cultivate deeper cultural, educational, and diplomatic connectivity directly in the heart of the UK capital. We bring together MPs, peers, and supporters dedicated to promoting a strong bilateral relationship and supporting peace in the Middle East.

Independent and non-partisan. Working across all parties.

Three areas. One relationship.

Explore our work

Diplomatic and policy dialogue

Hosting roundtables, briefings, and strategic discussions on the key issues shaping the relationship between the UK and Kurdistan.

  • Roundtables
  • Parliamentary briefings
  • Delegations

Culture, education and exchange

Deepening mutual understanding by connecting artists, academics, and students from both nations.

  • Cultural programmes
  • Academic exchange
  • Institutional connections

Trade and investment engagement

Unlocking new economic opportunities and building robust commercial ties between British and Kurdish markets.

  • Trade introductions
  • Business discussions
  • Commercial delegations

Strengthening the bond between the people of Kurdistan and the British people.

Our work rests on a relationship built over generations — founded on shared interests, mutual respect, and lasting friendship. We are here to give that relationship a true home and forward momentum.
